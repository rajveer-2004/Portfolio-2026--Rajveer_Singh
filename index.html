Overview

CSM Hub is an internal tool I built at WizCommerce that gives Customer Success Managers a pre-call brief and a client-health dashboard.

Challenge

CSMs spent about 35 minutes before every client call digging through HubSpot, Jira, Gmail and Gong.

Users

4 CSMs and their lead.

What I did

  • v1 posted AI updates into Slack. It was too noisy.
  • I sat with the CSMs and asked what they check in the 5 minutes before a call.
  • I rebuilt it as a pre-call brief and a client-health dashboard across 71 client domains, with LLM sentiment scoring and a risk leaderboard.
  • I added a data-cleaning step for missing fields and automatic Google token refresh.

Key decision

Fewer, better signals over more data.

Impact

35 → 3–4minutes of prep per account
4 of 4CSMs use it daily

What I owned

Discovery with the CSMs, the v1 to v2 product decision, and the build.

ToolsPython, Playwright, SQLite, XGBoost, Streamlit, GitHub Actions

Overview

A weekly pipeline that scrapes about 1,400 listings across 40 localities, predicts fair rent with XGBoost and flags underpriced deals on a live app.

Challenge

Renters can't tell if a listing is fairly priced.

What I did

Built the scrape, price model and flagging pipeline end to end, and shipped it as a live app that refreshes every week.

Key decisions

  • Regex beat Gemini Flash for field extraction (90.2% vs 89.2%, about 2,700x faster, free), so regex shipped.
  • I caught a "57% deal" that was 30 km away, and fixed locality tagging to protect trust.

Impact

115deals flagged per week
23.1%MAPE, down from about 33%
39 → 1,566unique listings after fixing a location bug
